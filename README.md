## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aemmadi/aemmadi/master/wave.gif" width="28"> Hi, I'm Abhishake

I'm an Associate Director in Salesforce Architecture at **LTM**, based in London. I've worked in CRM
for 15+ years, first on Siebel and now on Salesforce, and these days I spend most of my time on
**Agentforce** and **Data 360**. In my spare time I build small tools that make Salesforce delivery
a little easier.

- 🚀 &nbsp;Currently building **[Archistry](https://github.com/abhishakec/archistry)**
- 💬 &nbsp;Happy to talk Salesforce architecture, Agentforce and CRM delivery
- 🌍 &nbsp;English · Hindi · Bengali

## 🚀 Featured project: Archistry

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/abhishakec/archistry"><img width="560" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhishakec/archistry/main/.github/cover.png" alt="Archistry — Salesforce diagrams for AI agents" /></a>
</p>

<p align="center">
  An AI-agent skill that turns a plain-language request into an editable <b>draw.io</b> diagram,<br>
  an interactive <b>HTML</b> page and <b>PNG / JPG / SVG</b> images, for Salesforce projects.
</p>
